# 🍓 JUMP

My first ever game development project! 🎮

JUMP is a small 2D platformer where you play as **Pinkman**, explore a farm, collect strawberries, and finally reach the trophy. 🏆

This started as me just wanting to learn Godot... and somehow turned into an actual playable game. 😭

---

## 🎮 How to Play

Your mission:

🍓 Collect all **15 strawberries**  
🏆 Reach the trophy  
🎉 Complete the farm!

---

## 🕹️ Controls

### ← Move Left
Press the **Left Arrow (←)**

### → Move Right
Press the **Right Arrow (→)**

### ⬆️ Jump
Press **Space**

### 🚀 Jump Higher + Move Right
Press **Space + Right Arrow (→)**

### 🚀 Jump Higher + Move Left
Press **Space + Left Arrow (←)**

> ⚠️ **Important:** W, A, S, and D do NOT work.
> Use the **Arrow Keys + Space** to control Pinkman.

---

## 🏆 How to Win

First, collect **all 15 strawberries** 🍓.

Then make your way to the trophy. 🏆

When Pinkman touches the trophy **after collecting all the strawberries**, the **winning sound plays** and the completion screen appears. 🎉


## ✨ Features

- 🎮 2D platformer gameplay
- 🍓 15 collectible strawberries
- 🔢 Live strawberry counter
- 🏆 Trophy-based finishing system
- 🎵 Background music
- 🔊 Strawberry collection sound
- 🏆 Winning sound
- 🎉 Completion screen
- 🕹️ Jump + directional movement
